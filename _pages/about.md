I’m Enze Shi, a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics & Bioinformatics at [Duke University](https://scholars.duke.edu/person/enze.shi), where I work with [Prof. Anru R. Zhang](https://scholars.duke.edu/person/anru.zhang).

I earned my Ph.D. in Statistical Machine Learning (2026) and my M.S. in Statistics (2022) from the University of Alberta. My doctoral advisors were [Prof. Linglong Kong](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~lkong/) and [Prof. Bei Jiang](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~bei1/). I earned my B.S. in Computational Mathematics (2020) from Xiamen University, where I worked with [Prof. Chuanju Xu](https://math.xmu.edu.cn/info/1084/7691.htm).

I develop statistical methods for trustworthy machine learning and biomedical data analysis. My research spans inference for streaming and functional data, fairness and privacy in machine learning, and the generation and evaluation of synthetic data. I’m also interested in applications to medical imaging.
